# Oyera Auto Service Bay Ltd (OAS Bay) — Project Brief

Source: assignment brief, Refactory CSE — June Cohort 2026 (transcribed from lecturer's handout, 2026-08-28).

## Background

At the service bay station, heavy, small, and commercial cars come in for:

- Engine oil and filter change
- Gearbox oil and filter change
- Brake fluid change
- Brake pad change
- Greasing
- Other minor repairs

They also do wheel alignment and wheel balancing. A car can come in for one or more services in a day, and one technician **or more** can handle a single car's service.

They also sell service/repair parts on their own: filters, engine oil, gearbox oil, among others.

## Business Process

1. A car comes in.
2. A senior technician determines the parts and oils needed for the car, and the owner buys them.
3. The car is taken to a service bay for the work to be done.
4. A labour charge of **20,000 UGX** is charged for the service.

> Note: the brief only names a "senior technician" — it doesn't spell out a junior-technician handoff or job-card assignment. That structure (senior diagnoses → assigns a junior technician) came from your own description of how the shop actually runs, which is a reasonable real-world addition on top of this brief, not a contradiction of it.
